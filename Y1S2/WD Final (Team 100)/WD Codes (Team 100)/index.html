FAQs

Can I purchase tickets on the day of my visit?

Yes, but please note that tickets are subject to availability.
You may book your tickets here. It is also advisable to check on the park's operating hours on the day of your visit.

Are there any height or age restrictions for rides?

Yes, some rides have specific height and age requirements for safety.
Please refer to individual ride guidelines available at the park or on our website.

Are there facilities for guests with disabilities?

Yes, Doraemon SkyWorld is committed to providing an enjoyable experience for all guests. We offer accessible facilities and services.
Please contact Guest Services for assistance.

Is there a dress code?

Yes, appropriate attire is required. Guests should dress comfortably and modestly.
Offensive clothing or costumes that may be mistaken for park personnel are not permitted.

Can I bring my own food and beverages into the park?

No, outside food and beverages are not allowed in the park.
We offer a variety of dining options to cater to different tastes and dietary needs.

What should I do if I lose something in the park?

Visit the Lost and Found located at Guest Services.
You can also report lost items online or contact us via email.

Is re-entry allowed?

Yes, re-entry is permitted on the same day with a valid ticket.
Please ensure your ticket is scanned upon exit and re-entry.
