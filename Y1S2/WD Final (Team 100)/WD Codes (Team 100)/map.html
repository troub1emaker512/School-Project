Directions to Doraemon SkyWorld

By shuttle bus

Operating hours are from 9am to 7pm. Shuttle buses will arrive at the designated Metro stations every 30 minutes.

  • Take from Shinano station exit A which takes you about 30 minutes.
  • Take from Lizuna station exit C which takes you about 45 minutes.
  • Take from Otari station exit B which takes you about 60 minutes.

By Tongakushi-Jinja

Tongakushi-Jinja offers one of the most scenic cable car rides up to Doraemon theme park. Along the 15 minute ride, you can catch a beautiful scenery of mountains up to Doraemon theme park or you may alight and walk all the way up from Mount Tongakushi or Mount Takatsuma to enjoy the cooling breeze of the nature. You can also get off at Myoko-Tongakushi Renzan National Park to explore the beautiful flowers and heritage villages that are over 100 years.
